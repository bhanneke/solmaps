# Data and documentation licence

The solmaps code is released under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`).

The solmaps datasets and documentation are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
the resource atlas (`atlas/atlas_dataset.json`), the transfer data (`data/daily`, Parquet in the release), the computed route results (`flyby/*.json`,
`network/*.json`, `surrogate/*.json`), the documentation pages in `site/` and the built pages in `docs/`.

Please cite: Björn Hanneke, solmaps: the open engine of Deep Space Strategy, a temporal transfer graph of the solar system, https://github.com/bhanneke/deep-space-strategy.

Third-party data keep their own terms: JPL ephemerides and Small-Body Database elements (NASA/JPL),
and the sources cited per entry in the atlas.
