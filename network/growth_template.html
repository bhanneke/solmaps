Growth Map

Transfer costs between the atlas sites change with the planets' positions. This map recomputes them for every departure date from November 2026 to 2100, ranks propellant hubs by how much Earth-launched mass they save, and searches for depot orbits that reach many destinations cheaply.

Experimental research prototype. Part of Deep Space Strategy, built on the solmaps engine. Transfers use impulsive burns and patched conics, flyby routes come from a global optimiser, and the results are validated against several real missions, so the numbers support comparisons between routes, dates and hubs. Strongholds: propellant leverage

A hub with local water can refuel ships, so the propellant for the onward leg never leaves Earth. Leverage = average share of Earth-launched mass saved for the other sites when cargo refuels at the hub, compared with flying direct from low Earth orbit. The calendar moves the ranking.
